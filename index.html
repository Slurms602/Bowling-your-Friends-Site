Beat 'em. Ball 'em. Bowl 'em.

1. Brawl

Jab, jab, COMBO! Wind up a haymaker, dive-tackle the bowler, grab someone and yeet them across the alley.

2. Ball

Hit zero health and you curl up into a ball, seeing stars. Grab them fast, before they mash their way awake!

3. Bowl

Carry them to the red line, wind up, let go. Strikes, spares and a live alley scoreboard. Most points when time runs out wins.

Why it's so dumb (and so good)

Wobbly party physics

Jelly-bean bowlers that stagger, flop, tumble and go flying, just like a Gang Beasts night.

Punches that land

Combo finishers, full-charge SMASHes, dive tackles and throws. Every hit pops, shakes and squishes.

Real bowling rules

Strikes, spares, gutter balls and fouls. Bowl from past the line and you score nothing, so the line is where the fights happen. Play the neon Cosmic Lanes, or the rooftop Sunset Lanes, where bigger pins make strikes harder.

8 players + bots

Online with your friends, and bots fill the empty spots. Also great for warming up alone.

Make it yours

Viking helmets, afros, googly eyes, tuxes, flame shirts, boxing gloves... mix & match your bowler.

Painless to join

Type a 4-letter room code (or an IP). Friends on the same Wi-Fi just click your game in the list.

Play with friends in 3 steps

  1. 1

    Download & open

    Download the zip, right-click it → Extract All, then double-click Bowling your Friends.exe.

  2. 2

    One person hosts

    Click HOST GAME and tell everyone the code (or IP) shown at the top of the lobby.

  3. 3

    Everyone else joins

    Click JOIN GAME, type the code, done. Same Wi-Fi? Your friend's game is already in the list.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”? That's normal for small indie games. Click More info → Run anyway. If it asks about network access, click Allow.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yep. Download it, share it with your friends, bowl them.

Do all my friends need to download it?

Yes. Everyone needs the game, and everyone needs the same version, so if someone updates, everyone updates.

Can I play on my phone?

On Android, yes, and you can play against friends on PC. Download the APK on your phone and open it. If your phone asks, allow installing apps from your browser ("Install unknown apps"), then tap Install. Turn your phone sideways: left thumb moves, right thumb punches and grabs. No iPhone version yet.

Mac?

Not yet. Windows, Android and Linux for now.

It says it can't connect!

Double-check the code with your host. If the host is sharing an IP instead of a code, their router may need to let the game through (UDP port 7777). The game tries to do that automatically.

How many people can play?

Up to 8 per game. Short on friends? Add bots in the lobby.
